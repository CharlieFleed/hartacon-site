Battle Mechanics Guide


Stats

Each character has a set of innate stats:


SPD Speed, it affects the turns sequence.

STR Strength, it affects the damage of all physical attacks and some skills.

INT Intelligence, it affects the power of magic skills.

ACC Accuracy, it affects the damage of physical attacks with bows and knives and some skills.

DEX Dexterity, it affects the damage of physical attacks with knuckles, swords, spears, and staves and some skills.

VIT Vitality, it affects the overall Physical Defense value.

RES Resistance, it affects the overall Magic Defense value.


and a set of equipment stats that depend on equipped weapon, protections and accessories:


ATK Attack, it affects the damage inflicted by physical attacks and physical skills.

PDEF Physical Defense, it affects the damage inflicted by physical attacks and physical skills. Physical Defense is typically increased by Armors and Helmets.

MDEF Magic Defense, it affects the damage inflicted by physical attacks and magic skills. Magic Defense is typically increased by Hats.

EVA Evasion, it affects the possibility to evade physical attacks and physical skills. Evasion is typically increased by Shields.


Attacks

In order to succeed, attacks must hit and must not be evaded.

Hit Rate

The success-rate of an attack depends on the Hit RateHero of the attacker, which can be affected by altered states, and on the Hit RateWpn of the equipped weapon, which normally is 100% and goes down to 50% when the weapon is broken.

Evasion

Only close-combat attacks can be evaded, ranged attacks (Bows and Knives) can't.

In close combat, the chance of evasion is determined by the target's EVA, by the comparison of DexterityHero of target and attacker, and finally by the direction of the attack.

When DexterityHero values are equal, the chance of evasion equals the value of EVA; when the attacker’s DexterityHero is higher, the chance of evasion decreases, and vice versa.

As for the direction of the attack, the chance of evasion from the sides is halved; for attacks from behind it is reduced to one fourth.

Damage

The damage inflicted by a physical attack is calculated with the formula:

Damage = POWAttack - PDefHero

Counterattack

When attacked from the front or from the sides, a character that is not guarding will counterattack if his weapon has the proper range to hit the attacker. However, a character will only counterattack once until his/her next turn.

Attack Power

Attack Power (POW) depends on the attacker's primary stats and on the equipped weapon's Attack and its scaling factors. The formula is:

POWAttack =

ATKWpn +

STR_FWpn * STRHero+

DEX_FWpn * DEXHero+

ACC_FWpn * ACCHero

Physical Defense

Physical Defense depends on a character's Vitality and on the sum of equipped protections' Physical Defense. The formula is:

PDefHero =

VITHero +

Sum(PDefProt)

Skills

The damage inflicted by a skill follows the formula:

Damage =

POWSkill +

ATK_FSkill * ATKWpn +

STR_FSkill * STRHero +

INT_FSkill * INTHero +

RES_FSkill * RESHero +

ACC_FSkill * ACCHero +

DEX_FSkill * DEXHero +

VIT_FSkill * VITHero +

SPD_FSkill * SPDHero +

- MDefHero


Magic Defense

Magic Defense depends on a character's Resistence and on the equipped protections’ Magic Defense. The formula is:

MDefHero =

RESHero +

Sum(MDefProt)

Status Ailments

Status ailments inflicted by equipped gears cannot be cured.

Some status ailments are infective. If a character with an active infective ailment uses an attack or a physical skill against a target, a two-units fill effect will be added.

Filling of already active ailments has no effect.


Protections

Broken protections have halved PDefProt and MDefProt, and lose their auto-state characteristics.


Elemental damage

Protections' Elemental Damage, when positive, indicates the damage factor for an element, 100 indicating no change; when negative, it indicates an absorption factor.

Positive elemental damage values are added, not multiplied, the same for negative ones. A sum of positive reduced damage cannot give absorption effect (see Example 2).

When combining damage and absorption, the two values are multiplied.


Example 1

helmet elemental damage 120  +20%

shield elemental damage 70  -30%

ring elemental damage: -30  absorb 30%

COMBINED EFFECT:  [100 + ( 20 - 30 )] * ( -30 / 100)==>  absorb 27%


Example 2

helmet elemental damage 50  -50%

shield elemental damage 25  -75%

COMBINED EFFECT:  100 + ( - 50 - 75 ) = - 25==> 0%
